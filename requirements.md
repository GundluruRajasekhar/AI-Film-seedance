# Requirements – The Price of Love (HTML Film, v5)

## Goal
Free 7:00 cinematic web film (HTML, CSS, JS only) where the audience bonds with the characters. Lakshmi, Arjun's mother, is the emotional core.

## Characters
Arjun (28, teacher, honest, kind); Lakshmi/Amma (widow, tailor, unconditional love); Priya (girlfriend, pressured by family); Priya's Father (businessman, salary = success); Priya's Mother (reputation-driven); Google Director (society's success); Relatives (ignore him poor, respect him rich).

## Scenes (11, 7:00 total)
Opening 0:15 · The Invisible Man 0:40 · Respect Has a Price 0:40 · Love Without Status 0:30 · Rain rejection 0:40 · Mother's Sacrifice 0:50 · The Age of AI 0:30 · The Rise (company visuals, interview) 0:40 · The Truth (receipt, flashback) 0:55 · Hospital 0:40 · Final Speech 0:40

## Functional
1. Same engine as v1: narration via browser speech, subtitles with speaker names, generated music, timeline with scene markers, Play/Pause/Restart, Music/Voice/Subtitle toggles.
2. Character lower-third captions per scene and a Cast list under the player.
3. Rain effect (rejection scene), ₹50,000 caption (sacrifice), receipt caption and sepia flashback (twist), heartbeat line (hospital).
4. Higher pitch for female voices; slower, softer delivery driven by line length.

## v3 features
1. Voice picker (prefers English-India, Telugu or Hindi voices when installed).
2. Jump-to-scene menu and keyboard shortcuts (Space, F, M, V, C, L, arrows).
3. Fullscreen mode for clean screen recording; optional cinematic letterbox bars; film-grain overlay.
4. Scene image upload (local, never leaves the device) with slow Ken Burns zoom.
5. Emotion meter that rises toward the mother's sacrifice, twist and hospital climax.
6. End credits screen with Replay.
7. Download subtitles as .srt for CapCut.

## v4 story and sound design
1. Dramatic irony kept: viewers see Lakshmi sell the chain (Scene 6) while Arjun does not know.
2. The flashback in The Truth now shows new details instead of repeating the sale: no chain at his graduation, skipped meals, sewing through the night.
3. Music timing per line: near silence on "I'll manage", swell at the receipt, quiet in the hospital, full silence before "But only my mother asked if I had eaten."
4. Deliberate silent beats (2-4 s) after "I'll manage", the receipt, "I earned everything" and "When I became rich, they asked for my time."

## v5 photos and characters
1. Your photos are embedded inside index.html (base64), so the project stays four files: Arjun, Lakshmi (Amma), Priya, the businessman, office towers, a red sports car and a luxury car.
2. Characters appear on screen with name labels in the scenes they belong to; Arjun and Lakshmi are cut out, Priya and the businessman appear as framed portraits.
3. Uday, Arjun's friend, is added (drawn in the same cartoon style): he comforts Arjun in the rain and stays in the hospital.
4. Offices and cars cross-fade as backgrounds in The Invisible Man, Respect Has a Price, The Age of AI, The Rise and The Truth.
5. Cast gallery under the player with every character's picture.
6. Story, scene timings (7:00), music and voice are unchanged.

## Non-functional
Four files only; index.html self-contained; responsive 16:9; keyboard accessible; reduced motion respected.

## Export
Screen-record with system audio; edit in CapCut; export 1080p MP4.

Rule: at least 70% of the film (4:54 of 7:00) must be raw, unedited Seedance clips. 34 clips of about 10 s give about 5:40, which is 81% of 7:00. If Seedance clips are 5 s, generate two per shot.
